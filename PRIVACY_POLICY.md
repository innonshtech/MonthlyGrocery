# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 29, 2026  
**Applications Covered:** MonthlyGrocery (Customer App) and MonthlyGrocery Partner / EVER MERCHANT (Merchant App)

---

## 1. Overview
Welcome to **MonthlyGrocery**. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our applications collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile applications, web platforms, and grocery delivery services.

By accessing or using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
- **Name and Contact Details:** When you register or log in, we collect your phone number and full name to create and manage your account.
- **Delivery Addresses:** Street address, building/apartment details, locality, city, and postal code for delivery routing and fulfillment.

### B. Location Data
- **Approximate & Precise Location:** With your explicit permission, we access your device's location to:
  - Find nearby partner grocery stores and show available stock.
  - Accurately calculate delivery fees and estimated delivery times.
  - Enable real-time order tracking.

### C. Photos and Camera
- **Camera Access (Optional):** Used for merchant store registration, KYC document verification (Aadhaar, FSSAI, PAN), and customer profile picture updates.

### D. Transaction and Order Information
- Items purchased, order history, scheduled delivery slots, and payment status (payments are securely processed via certified third-party payment gateways; we do not store full credit card numbers or UPI PINs).

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:
1. Process, fulfill, and deliver your grocery orders accurately.
2. Authenticate user accounts via secure SMS OTP.
3. Provide customer support, order status notifications, and delivery updates.
4. Prevent fraudulent activities and maintain platform security.
5. Improve user experience and optimize catalog availability.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We respect your privacy and **do not sell or rent** your personal data to any third party. Data is only shared in the following necessary circumstances:
- **Delivery & Store Partners:** Only necessary delivery contact and address details are shared to fulfill orders.
- **Service Providers:** Cloud hosting (AWS), database storage (PostgreSQL/Supabase), and SMS delivery services (Twilio) strictly for operational purposes.
- **Legal Compliance:** When required by law or to protect user safety.

---

## 5. Data Security
We implement strict industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including:
- Data encryption in transit (HTTPS / SSL / TLS 1.3).
- Role-based access control and token-based authentication (JWT).
- Secure cloud storage with restricted permissions.

---

## 6. User Rights & Account Deletion
You have complete control over your personal information. You have the right to:
- Access and update your profile information in the app.
- Request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data.

To request account and data deletion:
- Use the in-app **"Delete Account"** option located under *Profile / Settings*.
- Or submit a request via our [Account Deletion Request Page](https://github.com/innonshtech/MonthlyGrocery/blob/main/DELETE_ACCOUNT.md) or email us at **monthlygrocery7@gmail.com**.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy
Our applications are intended for general consumer audiences and users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
