6. Payment Gateway Integration

Online Payment Processing (UPI, Cards, NetBanking, AutoPay)

🚫 NOT IN SCOPE FOR PHASE 1 (Deferred to Phase 2)
📋
Phase 1 Operating Model (Cash on Delivery / Direct Store Settlement):
For the Phase 1 Initial Launch, online payment aggregator integration (Razorpay / PhonePe / Cashfree) is NOT in scope. Orders will be processed using Cash on Delivery (COD) / Pay on Delivery / Direct Store Settlement. No payment gateway business KYC, merchant aggregator accounts, or webhook secrets are required for Phase 1 deployment.
Phase 2 Target Service Supported Modes Phase 1 Status Phase 2 Roadmap

9. Mandatory Legal Policies & Store URLs

Must be published on live public web URLs before submitting apps to App Store & Play Store

Required for Store Approval

1. Privacy Policy Webpage

Discloses data collection (phone number, GPS location for grocery delivery, device telemetry, analytics).

2. Terms & Conditions (EULA)

Defines minimum order thresholds (₹3,000 limit), recurring monthly basket rules, and delivery commitments.

3. Return, Refund & Cancellation

Specifies rules for damaged perishable items, wrong product deliveries, and refund turnaround timelines.

4. Shipping & Delivery Policy

Details operational delivery slot windows (Morning 7-10 AM, Evening 5-9 PM) and serviceable areas.

5. Public Web Account Deletion Request URL (Mandatory by Apple & Google)

Live webpage where registered users can request account & data deletion without installing the mobile app.
