## How to Delete Your Account and Data

Users can request permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data using either of the following methods:

---

### Option 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)
1. Open the **MonthlyGrocery** or **Merchant** app on your device.
2. Go to **Account** or **Profile / Settings**.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Delete Account"**.
4. Confirm your deletion request.
5. Your session will be immediately terminated, and your account marked for permanent deletion.

---

### Option 2: Request via Email / Web Support Form
If you no longer have access to the app or have uninstalled it, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

- **Send an email to:** `monthlygrocery7@gmail.com`
- **Subject line:** `Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Phone Number]`
- **Body:** Please include:
  - Your registered Mobile Number (e.g., `+91 XXXXXXXXXX`)
  - Your Name
  - Confirmation that you want all associated account records and addresses deleted.

---

## Data Deletion Timeline & Data Types Deleted

Upon receiving your account deletion request:

### 1. Data Permanently Deleted:
- User profile (Name, phone number, email, avatar).
- Saved delivery addresses and geographical location records.
- Saved cart baskets and customized preferences.
- Authentication tokens and device identifiers.

### 2. Retention for Legal & Financial Compliance:
- Completed transaction records and order invoices will be retained only for the legally mandated period required under Indian financial and tax laws, after which they are permanently purged.

**Processing Time:** In-app requests take effect immediately. Email requests are verified and processed within **48 to 72 business hours**.
